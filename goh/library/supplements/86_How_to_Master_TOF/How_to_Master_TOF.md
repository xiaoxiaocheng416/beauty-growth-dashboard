### 提取原文

**1. TOF Not Working / Not Enough New Eyeballs**

Many clients report that their top-of-funnel content is not generating reach, failing to attract new followers, or attracting the wrong people.

#### Mastering TOF content

**although we're building a personal brand about ourselves doesn't mean we should be posting everything about our lives**

i.e

if you're selling fitness but you like music doesn't mean you have to make content about playing the guitar or singing a song - it doesn't resonate

#### That is what unintentional content looks like

you have to make content for your ideal viewers best interest which means you must always relate to the vehicle or end goal that they have

the reason why content is made unintentionally is because people think that the DF and ICP are the 2 completely different people

**图中文字：**

They are the same people

| ICP | DF |
| --- | --- |
| qualification levels | personal characteristics |
| financial | beliefs |
| occupational | interests |
| | struggles |
| | passions |

图示关系：ICP 与 DF 两个方框的箭头共同指向上方的同一个人物。

This is one of the biggest reasons why a lot of you are posting a shit ton of content and you're also not getting the right ICP leads come through or the completely wrong audience is coming through

so when creating TOF content always ask yourself **what will my ideal viewer get from this video?** what type of value will they get that deeply resonates with them?

value -> entertainment, insight, resonance, relatability etc

## 5. How to ideate TOF content

### 原始截图 05

![截图 05：How to ideate TOF content 流程图](/Users/borgrise/Desktop/codex/视频创作_重构版/01-内容生产/workspaces/GOH_SOP_Portal/outputs/goh-sops-crawler/2026-09-21/supplements/86_How_to_Master_TOF/images/05.png)

### 提取原文与箭头关系

#### How to ideate TOF content

主线：**DF → content research → SLA → Implement DF into the idea → execute → rephrase / reframe / reformat → test**。

附加箭头：DF 还直接指向 “Implement DF into the idea”；execute 分别指向 rephrase、reframe、reformat，三个分支再汇入 test。

| 节点 | 下方说明原文 |
| --- | --- |
| DF | identify dream follower and marketing angles you can get from them to feed ideas |
| content research | research outlier videos - don't just pull videos that you think are good - steal proven videos |
| SLA | don't just copy the video outright - how can you make it better |
| SLA 下方第二级便签 | get creative - look for inspo outside of your niche |
| Implement DF into the idea | how can you twist this idea to speak directly to the dream follower - do this and you make content for them |
| execute | 无额外说明 |
| rephrase | 无额外说明 |
| reframe | 无额外说明 |
| reformat | 无额外说明 |
| test | 无额外说明 |
