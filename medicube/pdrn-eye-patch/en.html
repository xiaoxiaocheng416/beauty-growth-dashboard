For copy and testing

What the next script should lead with, and what proof cannot be lost

This section is not for more analysis. It is just the practical handoff: which direction to write first, what people really worry about, what must stay, and what to avoid.

Control Order

  • Write the problem-led targeted fix first.
  • Write the new-drop / brand-demand version second.
  • Keep the strong CTA / stock-pressure version separate for promo windows.
  • Do not mix the routine / bundle version into the broad control.

Real Frictions

  • How to place it, and which side goes where.
  • Whether it can be used on the upper lid or smile lines.
  • Morning or night.
  • How long to leave it on.
  • Whether it slides.
  • Why it is not on the main site yet, and why TikTok Shop gets it first.

Must Keep

  • The first 3 to 5 seconds must show the product and the patch.
  • Keep at least one clear placement action.
  • Keep at least one texture / serum / no-slip proof.
  • Bring in the ingredient trio early, but do not overdo the science.
  • Give it a clear routine slot, not just benefits.

Avoid

  • Do not turn it into a generic skincare landing page.
  • Do not let it become only a 'finally here' announcement.
  • Do not sell only the luxury / self-care feeling.
  • Do not skip usage clarity.
  • Do not let bundle logic take over every version.
