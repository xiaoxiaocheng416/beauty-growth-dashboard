What To Copy

Why we do not start from a blank script
Definition

Copy the order before changing the idea.

Start with one winning video. Keep the order. Change only one line or one proof point at a time.

Why Ty Needs It
  • It reduces back and forth before filming.
  • It keeps the product job clear.
  • It tells him which lines must stay and which can change.
  • It makes output faster because every post is not a new idea.
  • It lets us review what failed: opening, proof, product, or link line.
